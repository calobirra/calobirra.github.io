Calobirra

How many beers are your steps?

Calobirra is a playful iPhone app that counts today's steps and shows their approximate calorie equivalent in a small beer, a 33 cl bottle, a glass of wine or a can of soda, with the drink illustrated. It's free, with an optional one-time Calobirra Plus purchase. The app interface is in Spanish.

For adults 18+. It is a calorie comparison, not an invitation to drink: walking does not offset alcohol, and the app never tells you how much you can drink or logs what you drink.
