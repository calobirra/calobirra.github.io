My steps don't show

On “Hoy”, tap “Conectar mis pasos” and allow Motion & Fitness. If you denied it, enable it in iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Motion & Fitness. If you use Apple Health, check in the Health app that Calobirra can read Steps.

Restore Calobirra Plus

Open the Plus tab and tap “Restaurar compras” with the same Apple Account. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Delete my data

Calobirra Settings › “Borrar datos locales”.
