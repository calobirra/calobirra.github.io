In short

Calobirra has no accounts, no server of its own, no advertising and no third-party analytics. What the app uses is processed and stored on your iPhone. The developer does not receive your steps, weight or preferences.

What the app uses

  • Steps and dates from iPhone Motion (Core Motion), to show your activity.
  • Step count from Apple Health, read only and only if you allow it. Nothing is written to Health.
  • Daily step history, stored in a local file excluded from backups.
  • The weight you enter, your chosen drink and settings, stored locally.
  • A summary of today for the Plus widget, stored in storage shared by the app and its widget on your iPhone. Background App Refresh may re-read today's steps on the device to keep the widget current.

No location, contacts, camera, microphone or photos. Drinks consumed are never logged.

Purchases

Calobirra Plus is bought through the App Store. Apple processes the payment; the app only checks with StoreKit whether your account owns Plus.

Sharing

Only when you tap Share, the app creates an image with today's steps and equivalent (no weight) and hands it to the destination you pick in the iOS share sheet.

Deletion and your rights

Settings › “Borrar datos locales” deletes history and preferences; deleting the app removes them too. To exercise your data rights, write to {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}. You may also complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (aepd.es).

This website

No cookies, analytics or third-party scripts or fonts. The hosting provider, {{HOSTING_PROVIDER}}, may log technical data such as IP addresses to serve and protect the site.
